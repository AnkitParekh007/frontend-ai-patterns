# Recruiter Review Guide

## 30-Second Review Path

1. Open [README.md](README.md) for the positioning and architecture map.
2. Open [WHAT_THIS_PROVES.md](WHAT_THIS_PROVES.md) for the public proof summary.
3. Open [patterns/01-streaming-message-ux.md](patterns/01-streaming-message-ux.md) to see the cookbook format.

## 3-Minute Review Path

1. Read [docs/architecture.md](docs/architecture.md).
2. Scan [patterns/04-action-approval-flow.md](patterns/04-action-approval-flow.md) and [patterns/09-error-recovery-and-retry.md](patterns/09-error-recovery-and-retry.md).
3. Review [examples/typescript-models/pattern-models.ts](examples/typescript-models/pattern-models.ts) for typed implementation thinking.
4. Check [GOOD_FIRST_ISSUES.md](GOOD_FIRST_ISSUES.md) and [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) for project maturity and contribution direction.

## Best Files To Inspect

- [README.md](README.md)

- [patterns/01-streaming-message-ux.md](patterns/01-streaming-message-ux.md)

## What A Reviewer Should Notice

- the repo is useful to Angular and TypeScript developers, not just recruiters
- every pattern includes practical sections for failure, accessibility, and testing
- the content is explicit about frontend versus backend responsibility
- the examples stay mock-safe and do not fake product adoption
